### What's new

**Smart invert** — In a dark theme, the document color button has a menu: Automatic, Smart Invert, or Keep Original. Smart invert keeps the hue and reseats lightness onto the theme background and theme text. Faces stay in their original color, a little darker so they sit with the page. Turning onto a new picture is faster: one palette is converted once and later pictures look it up, and the face scan runs several blocks at once. A page already seen still comes from the cache. Simple invert is gone; a saved choice of it becomes Smart Invert. Light themes and Original document colors are unchanged.

**Enhance Display** — The wand stays with the book it was pressed on. After a restart, a pale scan is still on and other books stay off. Switching books follows that book's own state.

**Ebooks** — A contents entry in a boxed set opens that book. Repeated titles such as title page and copyright page no longer jump ahead. In a dark theme, a picture that is clearly a cutout on white paper loses that paper and shows the page background. Engravings, full photos, and uncertain pictures stay as they are, and pictures are no longer recolored into the theme as a group. Enlarging the font no longer cuts off the last character of a line. A fixed-width picture shrinks to the page, stays centered inside a centered paragraph, and is no longer sliced by the page edge. Underline, strikeout, and squiggly marks are drawn per line.

**Page wheel** — When the whole page already fits in the window, one wheel notch turns a page, even if neither Fit Width and Continuous nor Fit a Single Page is pressed. A page taller than the window still scrolls inside the page, then continues to the next page.

**OCR** — Automatic OCR no longer turns itself on for a scanned PDF. The toolbar switch is manual, and its on or off state is stored with that document. Automatic OCR only adds text; it no longer turns a page or straightens a tilt. OCR you start yourself still does. Manually Adjust Pages can turn by any angle, flip horizontally or vertically, and crop the empty corners. Copying a selection on an OCR'd scan puts the text on the clipboard.

**Read aloud** — Online multilingual is a choice plus a settings item, like the local and online English–Chinese modes. Choosing it uses the saved voice; settings picks which voice that is.

**Tables** — A document table's right edge is a full black line and meets the corners, like the other sides.

**Word** — Headings show the numbering stored on the style (`第1章`, `1.1`, `1.1.1`). A printed contents page draws the dotted leaders, and clicking an entry jumps to that heading. Those printed lines stay out of the sidebar.

**Bookmarks** — While bookmarks are being calibrated, the sidebar no longer shows a white strip at the bottom. The printed-page offset shows the full number (`+199` is no longer cut down to `+1...`). `1.1` nests under the open section, otherwise the chapter, otherwise the attachment. Switching tabs no longer crashes.

### 本版更新

**智能反色** — 暗黑主题里，文档颜色按钮有一个菜单：自动、智能反色、保持原色。智能反色保持色相，把明暗落到主题背景和主题文字上。人脸保留原色，并稍微压暗，和整页更合得上。翻到新图片更快：同一套主题色只换算一次，后面的图查表；找人脸的几块同时进行。已经看过的页仍用缓存。简单反色已去掉，以前选过它的会改用智能反色。浅色主题和「原版」不受影响。

**增强显示** — 魔法棒按下去只记在当前这本书上。关掉再打开，淡色扫描书还是按下的，别的书还是关的。换一本书，按钮跟着那本书变。

**电子书** — 套装里点目录会打开对应的那一本。重复的「书名页」「版权页」不再串到后面的书。暗黑模式下，白纸上已经抠好的插图会去掉这层白底，底下露出页面背景。版画、整幅照片和拿不准的图保持原样，插图也不再整张涂成主题色。放大字体后，行末的字不再被切掉。写死宽度的插图会缩进版心，居中段落里的图回到中间，也不再被页边切开一条。下划线、删除线和波浪线按每一行来画。

**滚轮翻页** — 页面已经完整显示在窗口里时，滚轮一格翻一页，不必先按下「适应宽度并连续」或「适合单页」。页面比窗口高时，滚轮仍在页内滚动，滚到底再进入下一页。

**OCR** — 自动 OCR 不再因为文件是扫描件就自己打开。工具栏上的开关完全手动，开或关记在这份文档上，下次打开还是这个状态。自动 OCR 只补文字，不再转动页面，也不再做倾斜校正。自己点的识别仍会转正。文件菜单里的「手动调整页面」可以按任意角度转动、左右或上下翻转，并裁掉转出来的空白三角。扫描页上复制选中文字会进剪贴板。

**朗读** — 「在线多语言语音」分成选定和设置两项，和本地、在线中英双语一样。选定用当前多语言语音，设置里再换具体的声音。

**表格** — 表格右边的线和其余边一样，是完整的黑线，角上接齐。

**Word** — 正文标题会带上样式里的自动编号（`第1章`、`1.1`、`1.1.1`）。目录页画出标题和页码之间的点线，点击一条会跳到对应标题。目录页上的条目不再混进侧边栏。

**书签** — 提取目录、校准书签时，书签栏底部不再露出一块白底。页差显示完整数字（`+199` 不再被截成 `+1...`）。`1.1` 挂在当前节下，没有节时挂在章下，再没有章时挂在附件下。切换标签不再崩溃。
